# ERIE

**Epistemic Reasoning & Investigation Engine**

ERIE is an evidence-first intelligence engine for building AI systems that produce transparent, explainable, and defensible conclusions.

Most AI systems begin with a model and ask it to reason.

ERIE begins with evidence.

Its purpose is to organize information into a structured body of knowledge that can be inspected, challenged, expanded, and trusted. Rather than treating information as isolated documents or embeddings, ERIE represents evidence, claims, entities, relationships, and time as first-class concepts.

## Why ERIE?

Modern AI is remarkably capable, but it often struggles to answer one critical question:

**"Why should I trust this conclusion?"**

ERIE was designed to solve that problem.

Every conclusion should be traceable back to the evidence that supports it. Every relationship should be inspectable. Every claim should be open to challenge. Every investigation should remain transparent.

## Core Principles

- Evidence-first reasoning
- Explainable conclusions
- Deterministic semantic architecture
- Temporal understanding
- Explicit entities and relationships
- Transparent confidence assessment
- Modular architecture
- Provider-independent design

## What ERIE Is

ERIE is an engine.

It is not an application, chatbot, search engine, or user interface.

Applications can build on ERIE to create investigative platforms, compliance systems, legal tools, healthcare solutions, financial intelligence platforms, and any other system where evidence and trust matter.

## Current Status

ERIE is under active development.

The project is focused on building a robust foundation before expanding into higher-level reasoning capabilities. Stability, correctness, and transparency take priority over rapid feature growth.

## Philosophy

Intelligence without evidence creates confidence.

Evidence without structure creates chaos.

ERIE exists to bridge the two.
